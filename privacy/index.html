Privacy

Your measurement files stay on your device.

BenchReport performs Touchstone parsing, analysis, charting, project creation, and report generation in the browser. The application does not transmit the contents of files you open.

Local information

Reusable rule templates are stored in your browser’s local storage. Installed/offline files may be held in the browser cache. You can remove these through your browser’s site-data controls.

Project files

When you save a .brp project, the browser creates the file locally. It may contain measurement data, names, report metadata, notes, and an embedded logo. Treat it according to the sensitivity of the data you placed in it.

Website analytics and hosting

The public site is delivered through GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy terms. BenchReport also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate visits, page views, referrers, countries, and site performance. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data. Measurement files and their contents are never sent to GitHub or Cloudflare by BenchReport.

BenchReport contains no advertising, account system, cross-site behavioural profiling, or third-party measurement upload.

Professional use

Only open or save data that you are authorised to process. The fact that processing is local does not override employer, customer, export-control, contractual, or information-security requirements.

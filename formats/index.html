Validation behaviour

Malformed numeric data, unsupported option lines, and negative or non-finite frequencies are rejected. Rows are sorted by frequency. If a file contains duplicate frequency rows, the final row is retained and a warning appears beside the measurement.

Known limitations

  • No interpolation at limit-band boundaries.
  • The 3 dB bandwidth calculation uses available sample points.
  • No de-embedding, calibration, time-domain transformation, or uncertainty calculation.
  • No guarantee of compatibility with vendor-specific content outside the public Touchstone structure.

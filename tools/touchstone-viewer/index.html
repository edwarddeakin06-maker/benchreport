Supported Touchstone data

BenchReport reads common Touchstone 1.0-style S1P and S2P files using DB/angle, magnitude/angle, or real/imaginary data. Frequency units can be Hz, kHz, MHz, or GHz. See the complete format and limitation list before relying on a result.

Your measurement contents stay local.

The selected files are parsed in the browser and are not sent to BenchReport or Cloudflare. Anonymous site-level analytics do not contain measurement data.

Touchstone viewer questions

Are my S-parameter files uploaded?

No. Parsing, plotting and acceptance calculations run locally in the browser.

Does it support both S1P and S2P?

Yes. S1P files provide S11; S2P files can provide S11, S21, S12 and S22.

Can I compare several measurements?

Yes. BenchReport Free overlays up to three measurements and lets you mark one as the golden reference.

Is BenchReport a replacement for a VNA?

No. It analyses existing Touchstone data and produces documentation; it is not a calibrated instrument or approved test process.
